OBJ Vertex Attrib to mesh block:
	OBJ file Blender param:
		Forward Axis : -Z
			 Up Axis :  Y
		
		UV Coordinates : ok
			   Normals : ok
		  Triangulates : ok

	VTN File Format:
		0[ vertex[ x, y, z ], normals[ x , y , z ], textures[ x , y ] ]
		1[ vertex[ x, y, z ], normals[ x , y , z ], textures[ x , y ] ]
		2[ vertex[ x, y, z ], normals[ x , y , z ], textures[ x , y ] ]
								***
		n[ vertex[ x, y, z ], normals[ x , y , z ], textures[ x , y ] ]
		
	this format not used IndexArray!
